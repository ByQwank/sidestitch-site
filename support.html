Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Include your SideStitch version, Chrome version, the affected platform and the steps that reproduce the issue. Support issues are public. Do not post passwords, session cookies or authorization tokens.

Connection problems

Connect Twitch using its authorization window. For Kick, sign in normally on kick.com, then use Connect Kick and allow optional cookie access. Kick uses an unofficial read-only website endpoint; platform changes or access restrictions can interrupt it. The extension does not open stream tabs to bypass failures.

Refresh and cached results

Opening or reloading a site refreshes stale data according to your cache setting. Ordinary tab switches do not fetch a new list. Use the sidebar refresh button for newer live status or counts. Cached rows show the last successful result, including through temporary connection failures.

Delete local data

Open Settings → Privacy → Clear local data. This resets settings, rules, cached channels and authorization. Disconnect removes the connection and channel cache while keeping preferences.
